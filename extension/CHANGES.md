# Skippy's Command Deck – what's new

The browser extension for https://your-command.adalia.academy and the Influence game.
Newest first. Get it from the [Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/pbiobnkpgohjcpednldpopbkedlhgpcd) – updates arrive automatically.

## 0.6.4 – coming soon
- **Fix:** the crew food bar now counts Dietitians and Food Preparation crewmates (Arvad Specialists get their extra half tier), so it matches the game's own food figure.

## 0.6.3 – 5 Nov 2026
- **Deck controls on the Command Deck** – the extension's settings are now on the Command Deck site too: click the glowing panel above the captain's chair on the Bridge to switch each game tweak on or off, pick the friend / foe ring colours and change or remove your tagged players. Everything is still saved inside the extension in your browser; the Options page works as before.
- **Lighter on the game** – the hide-used-deposits and auto-pick-sample helpers now only wake up when the game's screen changes, instead of checking the whole page several times a second. Helps the game load and run more smoothly.
- **Fix:** the crew food bar no longer sometimes shows under the game's top-left crew panel – it now only appears in the My Crews list.
- **Fix:** no more "Extension context invalidated" error after the extension updates while the game tab is open.
- **Fix:** the ship propellant bar no longer shows in a lot's Resources panel – it only appears in the My Assets › Ships list. (Reported by a player.)

## 0.6.2 – 4 Nov 2026
- **Friend / foe tags on the system map** (on by default – switch off in Options) – click an asteroid on the belt map and tag its owner as friendly, neutral or hostile, with an optional name. Every asteroid that owner has on the map gets a coloured ring (game green / yellow / red – change them in Options). Tags follow the owner, so a sold rock changes colour. Saved in your browser only; uses only what the game has already loaded.
  - **Your tag list in Options** – see everyone you've tagged, rename them, change or remove a tag.
  - **Share with friends** – export your list as a file; a friend imports it and sees the same friends and foes.
  - **Clear all tags** – start again if things get in a mess (asks first).
  - **Hover a ringed asteroid** to see the owner's name and tag.
  - **Tags switch** at the top of the belt map hides or shows the rings.
- **Open marketplaces on the system map** (on by default – switch off in Options) – a small green diamond (the game's Trade colour) on every asteroid with open market orders. Hover for how many marketplaces, items for sale and buy orders; click for each marketplace. A "Markets" switch at the top of the belt map hides or shows them. The list comes from the Command Deck's belt-wide market data, refreshed every 5 minutes – the only thing the extension fetches, and only from the Command Deck.
- **Ship propellant bar** (on by default – switch off in Options) – in the game's My Assets › Ships list, each ship has a propellant bar along the bottom with its %. Red at 10% and under, yellow around a quarter, green from half a tank. Hover it for the tonnes. Uses only what the game has already loaded.
- **Crew food bar** (on by default – switch off in Options) – in the game's My Crews list, each crew's card has a food bar along the bottom with its %. It fades from green when full, through yellow, to red at 50% (hungry). Uses only what the game has already loaded – no extra requests. (Asked for by a player.)

## 0.6.1 – coming soon
- **Status dots now work on big asteroids** like Adalia Prime – they line up with your buildings at every zoom, and no longer vanish when you zoom in. Also fixes dots landing on the wrong asteroid when two small ones are full of buildings.
- **Status dots switched on by default** – switch them off in Options if you'd rather not have them.
- **Green dot on planned sites** once every material for the building has been delivered – ready for you to start building.
- **"Refresh this page" message** – after the extension updates, the Command Deck now tells you straight away to refresh the page if an "in game ↗" link can't reach it, instead of the click doing nothing.
- **Auto-pick core sample** (on by default – switch off in Options) – when you open Extract Resource and no deposit is chosen yet, the top sample in the list is picked for you. You still press Extract yourself.

## 0.6.0 – 30 Oct 2026
- **Building status dots** (off by default – switch on in Options) – when you look at one of your asteroids, a tiny dot sits beside each of your buildings: green = ready (finished or idle), yellow = under 3 hours left, red = busy. Planned sites: yellow when the reservation runs out within 3 hours, red when it has run out with materials still in it. No dots on warehouses, tank farms, spaceports, marketplaces or habitats. Uses only what the game has already loaded – no extra requests.

## 0.5.1 – included in 0.6.0
- **Fix:** the Command Deck button no longer jumps into the Ship Inventory (or other panels) when you open a side bar menu.

## 0.5.0 – 30 Oct 2026
- **Options page** – choose which game tweaks you want. Right-click the crowned S in your browser toolbar › Options (or Extensions › Details › Extension options).
- **Hide used deposits** – when you pick a core sample to extract from, "Show Used Deposits" now starts unticked, so only untouched samples show. You can still tick it.

## 0.4.5 – 28 Oct 2026
- **Command Deck button in the game** – a crowned S at the bottom of the game's right-hand side bar takes you straight back to your Command Deck.
- **Lot colours stay on** – the Filter Lots › Building Categories colours are switched on every time the game loads, so no more clicking the paint icon.

## 0.3.2 – 25 Oct 2026
- First release on the Chrome Web Store.
- **"in game ↗" links** on the Command Deck open buildings, lots, ships, crews and asteroids in your open game tab without reloading the game.
- Click the crowned S in your browser toolbar to jump back to your Command Deck.
