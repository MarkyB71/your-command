# Skippy's Command Deck – what's new

The browser extension for https://your-command.adalia.academy and the Influence game.
Newest first. Get it from the [Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/pbiobnkpgohjcpednldpopbkedlhgpcd) – updates arrive automatically.

## 0.6.1 – coming soon
- **Status dots now work on big asteroids** like Adalia Prime – they line up with your buildings at every zoom, and no longer vanish when you zoom in. Also fixes dots landing on the wrong asteroid when two small ones are full of buildings.
- **Status dots switched on by default** – switch them off in Options if you'd rather not have them.
- **Green dot on planned sites** once every material for the building has been delivered – ready for you to start building.
- **"Refresh this page" message** – after the extension updates, the Command Deck now tells you straight away to refresh the page if an "in game ↗" link can't reach it, instead of the click doing nothing.
- **Auto-pick core sample** (on by default – switch off in Options) – when you open Extract Resource and no deposit is chosen yet, the top sample in the list is picked for you. You still press Extract yourself.

## 0.6.0 – 30 Oct 2026
- **Building status dots** (off by default – switch on in Options) – when you look at one of your asteroids, a tiny dot sits beside each of your buildings: green = ready (finished or idle), yellow = under 3 hours left, red = busy. Planned sites: yellow when the reservation runs out within 3 hours, red when it has run out with materials still in it. No dots on warehouses, tank farms, spaceports, marketplaces or habitats. Uses only what the game has already loaded – no extra requests.

## 0.5.1 – included in 0.6.0
- **Fix:** the Command Deck button no longer jumps into the Ship Inventory (or other panels) when you open a side bar menu.

## 0.5.0 – 30 Oct 2026
- **Options page** – choose which game tweaks you want. Right-click the crowned S in your browser toolbar › Options (or Extensions › Details › Extension options).
- **Hide used deposits** – when you pick a core sample to extract from, "Show Used Deposits" now starts unticked, so only untouched samples show. You can still tick it.

## 0.4.5 – 28 Oct 2026
- **Command Deck button in the game** – a crowned S at the bottom of the game's right-hand side bar takes you straight back to your Command Deck.
- **Lot colours stay on** – the Filter Lots › Building Categories colours are switched on every time the game loads, so no more clicking the paint icon.

## 0.3.2 – 25 Oct 2026
- First release on the Chrome Web Store.
- **"in game ↗" links** on the Command Deck open buildings, lots, ships, crews and asteroids in your open game tab without reloading the game.
- Click the crowned S in your browser toolbar to jump back to your Command Deck.
