Choose which game tweaks you want. Each one only changes how the game looks or what it shows you – none of them play the game for you.

In the game

Your tagged players
Import adds a friend's tags to yours – where you've both tagged the same player, theirs is used. On the belt map, the small "Tags" switch at the top hides or shows the rings, and hovering a ringed asteroid shows the owner's name.
