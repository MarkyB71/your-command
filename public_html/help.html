What it is

Skippy's Awesome Command Deck (also called Your Influence Command) is a free community website for players of Influence. It gathers everything you own across all your asteroids into one place: what's in your warehouses, which buildings are busy or idle, where your ships and crews are, and what you can make from the stock you already have.

The Skippy's Command Deck browser extension is an optional add-on. It joins the website to your open game tab, so a click on the Command Deck takes the game straight to that building or ship, and it adds a few small helpers inside the game.

It only reads. The Command Deck never signs, sends, buys or builds anything for you. It looks at your wallet address, reads your game data, and shows it to you. All the playing is still yours.

Getting started

  1. Go to your-command.adalia.academy. Use a desktop or laptop for the best view – phones work, but the big tables are easier on a larger screen.
  2. Click Step onto the bridge. Skippy's welcome plays the first time you visit.
  3. Click Connect / change wallet under the deck tabs. Pick your Starknet wallet (Braavos, Ready/Argent X and so on) – the site only reads your address. Or type in any wallet address instead.
  4. Wait for your game data to load. Big wallets with lots of buildings can take a little while – that's on purpose, to go easy on the game's servers.
  5. Once loaded, the green wallet button shows your address (for example 0x12c8…96bf). Click it any time to change wallet. The ✎ pencil beside it lets you give the wallet a name.
  6. Optional: install the browser extension so the in game ↗ links work.

Getting around

The site is laid out like a ship. Each deck is a room, and each console in that room opens one page on the big viewscreen.

  • Deck tabs along the top – The Bridge, Crew Quarters, Flight Deck, Mining, Engineering, Science Lab and Trade Deck. Each deck is one crew class and the game buildings it works in: the deck's colour is its crew class (Crew Quarters uses the Habitat's yellow, Trade Deck the Marketplace's green), and the Bridge stations show small dots in the game's own building colours.
  • Consoles – click one to open that page on the viewscreen. The page buttons under the deck tabs do the same.
  • ◂ Back button on the viewscreen (or the seat in front of it) returns you to the deck's start.
  • Rock picker – the drop-down at the top of the viewscreen picks which asteroid the page shows.
  • Search box – type a product, building or name to find it across your data.
  • The top bar – left: ▶ Play Influence. Middle: your SWAY, Deck controls (always in the centre) and Refresh Game Data. Right: search, Command Deck, your wallet and Help.
  • ⟳ Refresh Game Data – in the middle of the top bar, just right of Deck controls. It fetches the latest game data without reloading the page. Data also updates by itself every minute in the background.
  • Filter box above long tables – type to narrow the rows. Click a column heading to sort.
  • in game ↗ links beside buildings, lots, ships and crews – open that thing in your game tab (needs the extension).
  • Download at the bottom of the page – save your data as a file.

If you refresh your browser, the site takes you back to the page you were on.

The Bridge

The Bridge is the landing page – your daily starting point.

The captain's chair – your Captain's log

Click the chair to open your Captain's log on the viewscreen (click it again to close it). Its label shows how many notes you still have to do. The log has three tabs:

🗓 Today

Today's workload – the things that need your attention. Use the rock picker beside the tabs to see one rock, or All rocks:

  • Ready to collect – finished jobs waiting for you to collect the output.
  • Finishing soon – jobs that finish within 3 hours (the orange ones), with how long is left and the time. These are just for your information – they don't count as needing you.
  • Materials at risk – planned sites whose reservation is running out with materials still in them.
  • Waiting to accept – deliveries waiting for you.
  • Leases with less than 30 days left.
  • Construction – buildings being built, or finished and waiting for you to complete them.
  • Idle buildings – buildings doing nothing.
  • Storage nearly full – warehouses and tank farms 75% full or more.
  • Crews low on food and ships low on propellant.

📌 Skippys Sticky Wall

Your own to-do list as sticky notes – e.g. "Build a Habitat on TRCS CMS". Notes with no rock are white.

  • Type a note and click Add. Pick a rock beside it to tie the note to that rock – it takes the rock's colour and name. You can change the rock on the note itself.
  • Drag notes anywhere on the viewscreen. Scroll down for more room – drag a note to the bottom edge and the board grows.
  • Tick a note when it's done; ✕ removes it; Clear done removes all the ticked ones.
  • ✎ edits a note's text – press Enter (or ✓) to save, Esc to cancel, Shift+Enter for a new line.
  • 🔗 links two notes: click 🔗 on one, then click the other. A line joins them. Click a line to remove the link.
  • 📦 Still needed (on a note with a rock) – pick a building; a box linked to the note lists everything it needs, with what's still short on that rock in orange and ✓ for what's there (everything on the rock counts, including what's already in a planned site).
  • ⚙ Process (on a note with a rock) – search for a process and set how many runs; a linked box shows which building it needs (and whether you have one on the rock), what it makes, and what its inputs are still short. 🚚 Plan it in the Logistics Hub opens Production with the rock, product and amount filled in (only shown when the rock has the right building).
  • In a 📦 box, each item still short has make › – it opens Production in the Logistics Hub for that item. If the rock can't make it you see what's missing instead, e.g. ✗ needs a Shipyard. The hub sends nothing until you approve.
  • 🎨 Rock colours – pick a colour for each of your rocks from 10 of the game's colours.
  • While you work on the notes the Bridge consoles roll up to give them room. Move off the notes (or hover the Bridge consoles strip) to bring them back.
  • Notes, links and colours are saved in your browser only – nothing is sent to the server. They won't follow you to another computer or browser.

📜 Log

Everything your crews have done in the game, newest first, from the game's own records (the same as the Activity log). Each entry shows its Adalia Stardate – the game's own clock in Adalian days (one Adalian day is one real hour) – with the real time under it. The current stardate is at the top.

Click Skippy in the chair to hear his welcome again.

SWAY balance (top bar, left of Deck controls)

Once your wallet is loaded, the top bar shows how much SWAY is in it, with the game's SWAY icon – handy when buying across the belt. It reads your balance straight from Starknet and updates every 5 minutes; click it to read it again. It only reads – nothing is sent.

Deck controls (top bar, middle)

Click Deck controls in the middle of the top bar (beside your SWAY balance) to put the Command Deck extension's settings on the viewscreen: switch each game tweak on or off, pick the friend / foe ring colours, and change or remove your tagged players. Everything is still saved inside the extension in your browser. Needs the extension, version 0.6.3 or later.

The SkippyChain (gold console above the chair)

Pick any product and see the whole production chain – every step back down to the raw resources. For each part you can choose to mine it, make it, or buy it. Change how something is made on one card and every card for that same product in the chain changes too – e.g. set Iron to be made from Olivine once and all the Iron branches follow. Set Use my stock on to a rock and the chain takes your stock off first, so you only see what's still needed. The Still to produce – whole chain box adds up everything that has to be made, grouped by Refinery, Factory, Bioreactor and Shipyard.

The stations

Three stations each side of the chair – Crew Quarters, Flight Deck and Mining on the left; Engineering, Science Lab and Trade Deck on the right. Each shows its big number, any warning in red, and dots for its buildings. Click one to enter that deck.

The decks

Every deck has its own status page for its buildings – working, idle or full, and the time left.

👥 Crew Quarters

All crews · Habitat

Crews
A card for every crew: where it is, ready or busy, a food bar and its crewmates' pictures (class colours, name on hover). Click a crew to see all its members – picture, class and traits – with Full crew details ▸ for the old crew card. ◂ All crews or Escape takes you back. The orange 🍽 Feed crews button opens the Logistics Hub.
Crew planner
Try swapping crewmates between crews to see the best line-up. The controls and spare bench stay at the top while the crews scroll.
Habitat status
Each of your habitats and how many of your crews are stationed there.

🚀 Flight Deck

Pilot · Spaceport

Ships
Your fleet – where each ship is, propellant, cargo and crew on board. Status colours match the game: in orbit blue, in flight orange, docked white.
Travel
Trip times between rocks and the best launch windows.
Activity log
What your crews have done recently.
Spaceport status
Each of your spaceports and how many of your ships are docked there.

⛏ Mining

Miner · Extractor

Core Samples
Untouched samples, by rock or by resource.
Lot resources
The richest lots for each resource.
Resource Map
Pick a scanned rock and a resource and every lot lights up by how rich it is, in the game's own colours. "All resources" shows the best of each lot, and can highlight good free spots for warehouses and refineries. Add the rock's 3D model to see its real shape.
Supply chain
Which of your rocks can mine each raw resource, which need it flown in, and the quickest rock to fly it from.
Extractor status
Every extractor – working, finished or idle, what it's extracting and the time left.

🏭 Engineering

Engineer · Refinery, Factory, Shipyard and Warehouse – everything used when making products

Products
Your stock, product by product, across every rock.
Construction planner
What it takes to build a building or a ship on a chosen rock – what you have, what to fly in, and the fewest flights to do it.
Production planner
Make any amount (kg, t or kt) of a product on a rock. Tabs for Refinery, Factory, Bioreactor and Shipyard.
Engineering status
Every refinery, factory and shipyard job running, with the time left – soonest first.
Storage status
What's in each warehouse and tank farm, and how full it is. Switch between cards and a table.
Deliveries
Goods coming in, going out, and on the way.
Leases
Lease end dates for the lots you use.
Lot leases
Type any asteroid's name or number (or tap Adalia Prime or one of your rocks) to see its lease offer – price a day, minimum term and notice – and every lot on a globe: free to lease (green), leased by you (gold) or leased by someone else (grey). Hover a lot for who leases it, the price and when it ends. Click a lot to open it in the game. With Skippy's Command Deck 0.6.3 or later you can also colour leased lots by your friend / foe tags.

🔬 Science Lab

Scientist · Bioreactor – more to come with Discovery

Asteroids
Your rocks at a glance.
Spectral type raws
Which of the 22 raw materials can be mined on each asteroid type – handy when a rock comes up for sale. Pick one or more types and the raw materials found on them fill their test tubes in the game's colours. Pick a raw material to see which types have it. The types of your own rocks show "yours".
Bioreactor status
Every bioreactor – working, finished or idle, and the time left.

💰 Trade Deck

Merchant · Marketplace – trading only

Market
Buy and sell listings across the belt.
Trade finder
Spots where you could buy low and sell high.
Market history
Recent trades. Your crews show in gold; other players' crews show by name when they have given their crew one, otherwise as "Crew #number". Tick "Only my trades" to see just yours.
My trading
Your orders (are you cheapest, or top bid?), a watch list of products, and a calculator for what an amount sells for or costs right now, fees included.
Where to buy
The nearest places selling a product, with flight time, propellant burned and total cost. Choose Quickest or Economy.
Marketplace status
Each of your marketplaces and how many of your listings are on it.

🚚 Logistics Hub

The one page that can act in the game. Everything else on the Command Deck only reads. The Logistics Hub can send actions from your wallet – but only the ones you approve in your wallet.

Open it with the amber 🚚 Logistics Hub ↗ button in the deck tool row. You go through an airlock first, every time, with a warning: tick "I understand" and cycle the airlock. Tick "Remember that I understand" to skip the view out of the window next time (you still go through the airlock).

The Logistics Bridge

Inside, the hub is laid out like the Bridge, in orange: a viewscreen across the top and consoles below. Pick a console and its tool opens on the viewscreen.

📦 What needs doing (the chair)
Crews that could be fed, idle buildings, ships docked and deliveries on their way.
🏭 Production
Make something on a rock (below).
🚀 Cargo bay
Bring what a rock is missing in by ship (below).
🍽 Galley
Feed your crews: a card per crew with its crewmates and food; every crew that can be fed is ticked – click a card to untick it. Each is fed from the biggest Food stock on its own rock, up to a year's food. Busy crews start unticked.
🚚 Deliveries
How many deliveries are on their way – finish them in the game.
🗄 Storage
Storage tidy moves in here later.

🏭 Production

📋 Your production plans
Every plan you approve is saved (in this browser) and listed at the top of Production. Click one to see each process as ✓ done, ⚙ running (with its finish time), ✓ finished – collect it in the game, ▶ ready, or ⏳ waiting (and why) – then start the next ones from there. ✕ forgets a plan.
1 Rock · 2 What to make
Search by product, input or process – e.g. "Pyroxene" also lists what's made from it. Pick the product, and how much – a weight (kg / t / kt) for bulk goods, a count for things like modules. What's it for? fills in the amount from a building or ship recipe, e.g. Solids Automation Modules for 2 Factories. MAX fills in the most you can make from what's on the rock now, and says what runs out first.
3 What you need
Worked back through the chain only as far as the amount needs: what comes from storage, by-products, mining, and what has to be flown in or bought. The Inputs list is folded – click to open it. An in-between item that would need more than 5× its own weight flown in to make is flown in instead.
4 Processes to run
In order, inputs first – building type, free count, where the outputs go and the time.
5 Where things go
What it's for (Stock / Market / Building), the materials warehouse (inputs gathered and in-between products land here), stock, market and building stores and an optional tank farm for all-fluid goods – remembered per rock. Each shows how full it is now → when the plan is done (green / amber / red).
6 Moves · 7 Crews
Moves into the materials warehouse, nearest storage first. Two crews are picked: the quickest for these moves and the fastest for the processes – either can be fed first.
8 Start processes · 9 Approve
Start the ones whose inputs are already there, with each process's time and finish, and when it's all done. Approve once in your wallet. Very slow processes (e.g. Natural Flavorings) start only as many runs as the game allows in one go.

🚀 Cargo bay

Steps 1–3 as Production, on the rock you want to make it on. Then:

Fly it in
How many rocks you need to fly in from (quickest first), and anything not in stock enough – how much is short and which of your rocks can make it.
A card per rock
What it supplies, the flight time, the ship that's there or the nearest one (and how far away), how many trips, and when it'd all be done. Light and Heavy Transports only. Details – by material is folded.
Picking the ship
The best ship is picked again as the flight times come in – a ⚠ note says if it changed. If you pick one yourself and a quicker one turns up, it tells you. Full ships are marked and put last. Click a rock card to load its ship – the card says 📦 Click to load when a ship is there.
Load
When the ship is at that rock: exactly what goes into it (storage → ship, item, amount), the crew there, then approve. Otherwise it tells you which ship to fly there.

Every tool: your wallet shows each action before anything happens, and everything is checked again just before sending. It only loads ships – you fly and unload them yourself. After moves, finish them in the game with Finish All Ready Items, then press ↻ Check the game again.

The browser extension

Skippy's Command Deck is a small, free extension for Chrome, Brave and Edge.

Get it from the Chrome Web Store

Edge users: allow extensions from other stores when asked. Updates arrive by themselves.

What it does

  • in game ↗ links – click one on the Command Deck and your open game tab moves to that building, lot, ship, crew or asteroid, without reloading the game. If no game tab is open, it opens one.
  • Crowned S in the game – a button at the bottom of the game's right-hand side bar takes you back to your Command Deck tab.
  • Crowned S in your toolbar – click it from anywhere to jump back to the Command Deck.
  • Small game helpers – lot colours kept on, building status dots and more. See Extension options.

How to use it

  1. Install it from the store, then refresh any Command Deck and game tabs you already had open.
  2. Open the game in one tab and the Command Deck in another.
  3. Click any in game ↗ link on the Command Deck. The game tab comes to the front at that spot.
  4. Click the crowned S (in the game or the toolbar) to come back.

Tip: pin the extension to your toolbar (the jigsaw icon › pin) so the crowned S is always to hand.

Extension options

To open the options: right-click the crowned S in your browser toolbar › Options. Or go to Extensions › Skippy's Command Deck › Details › Extension options.

Tick or untick each one. Most changes apply when you reload the game tab. You can also change them on the Command Deck: Deck controls (top bar, middle).

Building status dots

When you look at one of your asteroids in the game, a tiny dot sits beside each of your buildings:

  • Green – ready: finished, or idle and free to use.
  • Yellow – under 3 hours left on its job.
  • Red – busy.

On planned sites: green once every material has been delivered (ready to build), yellow if the reservation runs out within 3 hours, red if it has run out with materials still in it.

Warehouses, tank farms, spaceports, marketplaces and habitats don't get a dot. The dots use only what the game has already loaded – no extra requests.

Ships in flight

Zoom out to the belt map in the game and every ship in flight shows where it is right now, worked out with the game's own flight maths:

  • Your ships – purple dots, with a faint purple line along each route.
  • Other players' ships – triangles in your tag colours: teal friendly, yellow neutral, red hostile, orange not tagged. Hostile routes are always drawn; hover any other ship to see its route.
  • A hollow ring marks where a ship and its destination rock meet. The rocks keep moving while the ship flies, so light blue dots lead from the ring back to where that rock is today.

Hover a ship for its name, owner, route, when it leaves (if it hasn't yet) and when it arrives. The Ships in flight switch at the top right of the map hides or shows them all. Other players' ships come from the Command Deck's belt-wide list, which updates every 5 minutes.

Safety & privacy

  • The website only reads your wallet address – except the Logistics Hub, the one page that can send game actions. It only sends what you approve in your own wallet, and only for the wallet the page is showing. You go through an airlock warning every time you enter it.
  • The extension can only reach two sites: game.influenceth.io and your-command.adalia.academy.
  • It never reads, stores or sends your wallet, keys, passwords, game data or browsing.
  • The only things it ever fetches are the belt-wide lists of open market orders and (from 0.6.5) ships in flight, and only from the Command Deck itself (for the system map). It sends nothing about you when it does.
  • Inside the game it only changes how things look. It never clicks, buys, signs or sends anything.
  • Your extension choices are saved in your browser only.
  • All the code is public on GitHub – the extension is small enough to read in a few minutes.

Problems & FAQ

The in game ↗ links don't show

The links only appear when the extension is installed. Install it, then refresh the Command Deck tab.

Clicking an in game ↗ link does nothing, or says "Refresh this page"

The extension has probably just updated. Refresh the Command Deck tab and try again.

My data is out of date

Click ⟳ Refresh Game Data at the top. Data also updates every minute by itself. A few things in the game take a little while to show up everywhere.

It's slow to load

The site goes gently on the game's servers, as Influence is still a young game. Big wallets take longer – it's worth the wait.

Status dots or lot colours aren't showing in the game

Check the option is ticked in Extension options, then reload the game tab.

Can I look at someone else's wallet?

Yes – click the wallet button and type in any address. Everything in Influence is on-chain and public.

Does it work on my phone?

The website does, though big tables are easier on a larger screen. The extension needs a desktop browser.
