ADALIA ACADEMY

INFLUENCE is an online strategy game set in Adalia, a star system with no habitable planets but a vast asteroid belt holding every mineral its colonists need to survive and thrive. The Academy helps new and old players find their way.

The Student Library

The Library gives Academy members reference and source material through their studies and on into their careers in Adalia. Its academics share their latest research, including in-depth studies of how class efficiency bonuses and traits change the time or yield of each action in the game.
